# Procurement Thresholds and Approvals

Purchases under 10,000 dollars may be made with a departmental acquisition card by any manager with delegated authority. Purchases between 10,000 and 40,000 dollars require three quotes and approval by a Director.

Contracts above 40,000 dollars must go through a competitive process posted on the government tendering service, unless an exception under the Government Contracts Regulations applies. Sole-source justifications above 40,000 dollars require approval by the Assistant Deputy Minister.

Professional services contracts that involve access to Protected B information require the supplier to hold a Designated Organization Screening before award.
