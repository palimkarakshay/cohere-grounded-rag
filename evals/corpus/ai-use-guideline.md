# Guideline on the Use of Generative AI

Generative AI tools may be used for drafting, summarizing and translation of unclassified material. Protected or classified information must never be entered into a tool that is not deployed within the department's own environment.

Every output used in a decision affecting a person must be reviewed by a human before use, and the use of the tool must be documented in the file. Departments should prefer models that can be deployed on premises or in a Canadian sovereign cloud when handling Protected B data.

A departmental AI register lists every approved tool, its deployment model and its approved data classification. New tools require a privacy impact assessment before being added.
