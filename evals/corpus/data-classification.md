# Data Classification Quick Reference

Unclassified information may be stored on any approved departmental system. Protected A information requires access control; Protected B information requires encryption at rest and in transit and must stay on systems accredited for Protected B.

Secret and Top Secret information may only be processed on accredited classified networks that are physically separated from the departmental network. Removable media is prohibited on classified networks without written approval from the Departmental Security Officer.

The department's enterprise AI workspace is accredited for Protected B. It is deployed on premises on Canadian soil and does not send data outside the departmental boundary.
