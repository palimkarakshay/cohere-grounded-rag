# Leave and Accommodation Procedures

Employees accrue 15 days of annual leave per year for the first 8 years of service and 20 days per year thereafter. Unused annual leave above 35 days is paid out at the end of the fiscal year.

Requests for workplace accommodation are submitted to the manager, who must respond within 10 working days. Medical documentation, when required, is retained by the Occupational Health unit and is never stored on the employee's personnel file.

Parental leave top-up is 93 percent of salary for up to 52 weeks, subject to the collective agreement.
