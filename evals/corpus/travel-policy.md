# Directive on Travel and Hospitality

Employees must book air travel through the departmental travel service at least 14 days before departure. Economy class is the default for flights under 9 hours. Business class may be approved by a Director General for flights of 9 hours or longer.

Meal allowances are paid at the National Joint Council rates. Receipts are not required for meal allowances but are required for every hospitality expense above 50 dollars.

Travel claims must be submitted within 30 days of the return date. Claims submitted after 60 days require written justification from the employee's manager.
