# Security Incident Response Standard

A suspected security incident must be reported to the departmental Security Operations Centre within one hour of discovery. The SOC assigns a severity from 1 (critical) to 4 (low) within two hours of the report.

Severity 1 incidents require notification of the Chief Information Officer and the Departmental Security Officer within four hours, and a preliminary report to the central cyber authority within 24 hours.

Affected systems may only be reconnected to the network after the SOC confirms containment and the system owner signs the restoration checklist. A post-incident review is mandatory for severity 1 and 2 incidents and must be completed within 30 days.
